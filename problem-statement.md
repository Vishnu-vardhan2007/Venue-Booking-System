# **CS 201 - Object Oriented Design, Analysis and Programming**

## **Assignment 01 - Problem Statement**

### **Project Title**

**Venue Booking & Management System**

### **Problem Statement**

Finding a suitable venue for a wedding, meeting, conference, party, seminar, sports activity, or other event can be difficult and time-consuming. People usually have to search for venues through different sources, contact venue owners individually, ask about availability, compare facilities and pricing, and wait for confirmation. This process often leads to delays, incomplete information, communication problems, and difficulty in keeping track of booking requests.

Venue owners also face difficulties in managing their venues and booking requests. They need to maintain information about each venue, including its capacity, location, facilities, pricing, and available dates. When several customers contact them for different dates, it becomes difficult to keep track of requests and avoid conflicts. Managing bookings manually through phone calls, messages, or other disconnected methods also makes it difficult to maintain an accurate booking history.

The Venue Booking & Management System provides a centralized platform connecting customers with venue owners. Customers can register and log in, search for venues based on requirements such as location, date, venue type, capacity, and price, and view important venue information such as facilities and pricing. They can check the availability of a venue and submit a booking request by providing the required event details and preferred date and time.

Once a customer submits a booking request, the system provides the relevant venue-owner contact details to the customer. The customer and venue owner can then communicate directly to discuss the booking. Following this discussion, the venue owner updates the booking request status in the system as accepted or rejected, and the customer is notified of the decision. Confirmed bookings are recorded in the customer's booking history so that the customer can easily track previous and upcoming bookings.

Venue owners can register and manage their venues, update availability, view booking requests, and accept or reject them. An administrator can manage users, approve or reject venue listings, manage bookings, and generate basic reports.

The proposed system therefore aims to make venue discovery and booking more organized, transparent, and convenient for customers, while providing venue owners with a centralized way to manage their venues, availability, and booking requests.
