OOP Concepts Mapping (Stages 1 – 3)

Direct correspondence to the 12-Week Syllabus (Completed up to Oct 15)

Stage 1: Core Classes & Encapsulation (W1–W2)

Classes: Venue, User, Customer, Admin, Booking.

Encapsulation: Private fields (#id, #basePrice, #bookedDates, #status) accessed via controlled getters & methods (checkAvailability(), bookDate(), cancel()).

File: oop/models.js

Stage 2: Inheritance & Polymorphism (W3–W4)

Inheritance: Customer & Admin extend User; WeddingVenue, BirthdayVenue, ConferenceVenue extend Venue.

Polymorphism: calculateTotalPrice() is dynamically overridden in each subclass to include event-specific pricing rules & feature sets.

Composition: Booking holds references to Venue and Customer instances (has-a relationship).

File: oop/models.js

Stage 3: Abstraction & Service Layer (W5–W8)

Abstraction: UI never accesses raw arrays directly; all operations pass through VenueSearchService and BookingService.

Method Overloading: VenueSearchService.search(...) handles multiple call signatures (keyword, category + location, or filter object).

Lifecycle: Full date availability verification, booking creation, and cancellation integrated with browser storage.

File: oop/services.js & venues.js
