Safety Guidelines

Shared standards for safe, compliant, and worry-free celebrations.

VenueVerse promotes safe events for guests, organizers, and hosts. Both event organizers and venue partners are expected to follow these core guidelines:

  • Respect Maximum Guest Capacity: Never exceed a venue's approved maximum guest limit. Crowd-safety and fire-exit regulations strictly prohibit over-capacity bookings.
  • Verified Property Details: Hosts must provide accurate photos, floor capacities, emergency exits, power backup details, and clear house rules before publishing a venue.
  • Emergency & Fire Readiness: All listed indoor halls and banquet spaces must keep emergency exits unobstructed and maintain accessible first-aid and fire-safety equipment.
  • On-Platform Bookings & Receipts: Keep reservations and status updates within VenueVerse so your booking ID, invoice breakdown, and host confirmation remain documented.
  • Prohibited Activities: Illegal activities, unpermitted pyrotechnics indoors, and unsafe structural setups without host consent are strictly forbidden.

Cancellation Policy

Clear refund rules when plans change before your event date.

We balance flexibility for organizers with schedule protection for venue hosts. Refunds depend on how early a booking is cancelled prior to the scheduled event date:

14+ Days Before Event 100% full refund of the venue booking fee paid on VenueVerse.
7 to 13 Days Before Event 50% refund of the venue rental rate, minus applicable service processing costs.
Less Than 7 Days Before Event Non-refundable for standard user-initiated cancellations unless approved by the host.
  • Host Cancellations: If a venue owner cancels or is unable to honor a confirmed reservation date, you receive a 100% full refund and priority support to rebook another space.
  • Date Modifications: Date changes are subject to venue availability on the new date and any applicable rate adjustments.

Privacy Policy

How VenueVerse collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

VenueVerse respects your privacy and collects only the details needed to operate accounts, verify venue listings, and process reservations.

  • Information We Collect: Name, email address, phone number, role (User or Venue Owner), booking event details (date, guest count, venue choice), and submitted venue reviews.
  • How We Use Your Data: Information is used to authenticate sessions, check real-time date availability, notify hosts about booking requests, and display verified reviews and ratings.
  • Limited Sharing: Relevant organizer contact details and event specifications are shared only with the host of the venue you request or book. We do not sell personal data to third-party advertisers.
  • Security & Rights: Account passwords are stored securely via backend hashing. To request account updates or data removal, contact support@venueverse.com.
